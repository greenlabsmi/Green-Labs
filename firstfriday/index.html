Before you visit

First Friday questions

Is the event free?

Yes. First Friday is free to attend, and no ticket is required.

Do I need to stay for the entire event?

Not at all. Stop by for a few minutes, visit the vendors, play a game, or hang out for the evening.

Will there be food or drinks?

Food and drink vendors are not currently confirmed for this month. Green Labs will have water available during the event.

What kinds of vendors will be there?

The lineup includes glass art, handmade jewelry, and other local makers, with more details and vendor profiles added as they are finalized.

Will there be giveaways?

Yes. Green Labs will be giving away promotional merchandise and complimentary items, especially through games and event challenges. Offers are available while supplies last.

Is cannabis consumption allowed at First Friday?

No. First Friday is a community vendor event and is not an onsite cannabis consumption event. Please do not consume cannabis on the event grounds. Cannabis purchases remain inside Green Labs and are available only to legally eligible customers.

Does First Friday happen every month?

Yes. First Friday returns on the first Friday of every month with new vendors, activities, collaborations, and surprises.
