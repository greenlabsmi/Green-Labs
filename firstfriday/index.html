Before you visit

First Friday questions

Is the event free?

Yes. First Friday is free to attend, and no ticket is required.

Do I need to stay for the entire event?

Not at all. Stop by for a few minutes, visit the vendors, play a game, or hang out for the evening.

Will there be food?

Food vendors may participate during future events, but food is not guaranteed for this month. Additional details will be posted as they are confirmed.

Will more vendors be announced?

Yes. A growing group of local vendors is expected, and more information will be added as it becomes available.

Does First Friday happen every month?

Yes. First Friday returns on the first Friday of every month with new vendors, activities, collaborations, and giveaways.
